Daily tip · Fri 9 Oct

Swap one sweet drink for water

Sweet drinks like es teh manis or soda add up fast. Swapping just one a day for water is an easy win.

Water hydrates you with no sugar. If plain water feels boring, add lemon, mint or a few slices of cucumber.

WHO recommends keeping free sugars below 10% of your daily energy, and says going under 5% brings extra benefits.

Source: WHO — Healthy diet fact sheet
